## 2. Jobs To Be Done (JTBD) Progress Matrix

### Core Job Statement
> *"When I [Situation/Context], I want to [Motivation/Progress], so that I can [Desired Outcome/Transformation]."*

### The Multi-Dimensional Job Breakdown:
1. **Functional Job**:
   - *What is the practical task?*
   - *Example*: Find a fund where I can invest Rs. 2,000/month with reasonable long-term growth and minimum hassle.
2. **Emotional Job (Personal)**:
   - *How do they want to feel? What feeling are they trying to avoid?*
   - *Example*: Feel confident and self-reliant; avoid feeling naive, reckless, or vulnerable to financial disaster.
3. **Social Job (Interpersonal)**:
   - *How do they want to be perceived by colleagues, friends, or family?*
   - *Example*: Be viewed as an intelligent, responsible adult who manages finances prudently.

## 3. The Four Forces of Progress Matrix

| Force | Description | User Signals & Observations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Push of the Current** | What makes their current state intolerable or frustrating? | e.g., Inflation eroding bank savings, peer pressure seeing friends invest. |
| **Pull of the New** | What attracts them to the discovery experience? | e.g., Promise of beating inflation, compounding wealth, ease of direct funds. |
| **Anxiety of the New** | What fears, uncertainties, and doubts freeze them? | e.g., "What if the market crashes tomorrow?", "What if I can't withdraw my money?", "Are there hidden charges?" |
| **Habit & Inertia** | What comfortable, existing behaviors keep them stuck? | e.g., "It's easier to just leave it in my salary account or make another Fixed Deposit." |
