# Deep Dive: Teresa Torres on Continuous Discovery & Story-Based Interviewing

*Source: "The #1 Skill PMs Need in 2025: AI Product Discovery Masterclass by World's Leading Authority" (Interview with Aakash Gupta, featuring Teresa Torres, author of Continuous Discovery Habits)*

---

## 1. Core Paradigm: Continuous Discovery Habits

Traditional product development isolates research into sporadic, heavy-weight projects (e.g., quarterly research sprints). By the time products ship, findings are stale.

Teresa Torres redefines discovery as:
> **"At a minimum, weekly touchpoints with customers By the team conducting discovery (Product Manager, Designer, Tech Lead) where they conduct small research activities in pursuit of a desired outcome."**

### Key Tenets for an AI Discovery Engine:
- **Continuous Rhythm**: Empathy is not a one-and-done milestone; user sentiment and friction evolve.
- **Trio Ownership**: Discovery is owned collaboratively by Product, Design, and Engineering to build shared intuition.
- **Outcome Anchoring**: Discovery starts with an agreed-upon target outcome (e.g., "Increase new user activation confidence"), NOT with a feature backlog.

## 2. Story-Based Customer Interviewing: The Cure for Speculation

One of the greatest hazards in product discovery is **speculative questioning**. When asked hypothetical questions ("Would you like X?", "How much would you pay for Y?", "What features do you want?"), users:
1. Try to be polite and agreeable ("Sure, that sounds cool!").
2. Poorly predict their future behavior (humans are notoriously inaccurate at forecasting their own future actions).
3. Rationalize their decisions retroactively rather than recalling true motives.

### The Story-Based Protocol
Instead of asking users what they want or think, ask for **specific episodic stories of past behavior**:

| ❌ Flawed / Speculative Inquiry | ✅ Story-Based Episodic Inquiry |
| :--- | :--- |
| "What features do you look for when choosing a mutual fund?" | "Tell me about the last time you chose to invest your money in a new mutual fund. Where were you? What prompted you to look?" |
| "Do you think our risk score is clear?" | "Walk me through what happened the last time you saw that risk rating. What did you do next?" |
| "Would you prefer a comparison table or a graph?" | "When you were deciding between Fund A and Fund B, how did you actually compare them? Did you write anything down? Show me what you did." |

### Listening for Underlying Empathy Cues
During story-based inquiry, listen specifically for:
- **Moments of Hesitation**: Pauses, doubts, tab-switching, or asking someone else for advice.
- **Emotional Frustration**: Words indicating dread, intimidation, distrust, or fatigue.
- **Unspoken Workarounds**: How the user bridged gaps when existing tools failed them (e.g., copying numbers into Excel, texting a knowledgeable friend, searching Reddit).

## 3. The Opportunity Solution Tree (OST)

Torres’ Opportunity Solution Tree is the foundational architecture for mapping user empathy into actionable opportunity spaces:

```
               [ Desired Product Outcome ]
                           │
       ┌───────────────────┴───────────────────┐
       ▼                                       ▼
 [ Opportunity 1 ]                       [ Opportunity 2 ]
 (Major Unmet Need)                      (Major Unmet Need)
       │                                       │
  ┌────┴────┐                             ┌────┴────┐
  ▼         ▼                             ▼         ▼
[Sub-Opp] [Sub-Opp]                     [Sub-Opp] [Sub-Opp]
```

### Strict Definition of an "Opportunity"
An **Opportunity** is:
- An **unmet customer need, pain point, or desire**.
- Expressed **in the customer's own language and perspective**.
- **Completely devoid of any specific solution, feature, or mechanism.**

### Common Errors to Avoid in the Discovery Engine:
- **Masking features as opportunities**:
  - ❌ *"Opportunity: Users need a SIP calculator widget."*
  - ✅ *"Opportunity: Users are anxious that they don't know how much money they will accumulate over 5 years given inflation."*
- **Mixing levels of abstraction**: Group granular pain points under coherent parent opportunity branches so teams can prioritize strategically.

## 4. The Role and Guardrails of AI in Discovery

In her 2025 masterclass, Torres addresses how AI transforms discovery workflows:

### Where AI Excels:
1. **Qualitative Clustering**: Ingesting hundreds of interview transcripts, customer support threads, app store reviews, and forum discussions to detect recurring themes.
2. **Latent Sentiment Detection**: Highlighting subtle emotional shifts (e.g., identifying when user language transitions from curious to overwhelmed).
3. **Drafting Opportunity Hypotheses**: Rapidly structuring raw secondary signals into an initial Opportunity Tree.

### Critical AI Guardrails & Limitations:
1. **Never Hallucinate User Empathy**: AI cannot replace real human emotion or invent customer pain out of thin air. Every opportunity surfaced by AI must trace back to verifiable customer signals or quotes.
2. **AI Findings are Secondary Hypotheses**: AI-generated opportunities are not final truths; they serve as **secondary research scaffolding** that instructs what specific stories to investigate during primary user interviews.
