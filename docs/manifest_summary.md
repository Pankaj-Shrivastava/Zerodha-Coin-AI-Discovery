## 6. How this was built and checked

- Tags: in-session reading with an exact evidence span per tag; human gold set and a 40-record review round applied; gold labels override AI tags.
- Problems: friction labels grouped into problems (`theme_map.json`); every label used maps to exactly one problem.
- Build checks (all passed, or nothing is written): counts recomputed independently; channel and platform splits sum to the count; every quote is an exact substring of the raw record and contains its evidence span; generated text cites ≥3 posts from its own evidence, contains no digits, % or quotation marks; question status lowered only with a reason; interview questions are episodic; problem cards and research tracks share the same invalidation text.
- Bias controls: denominators and channel split on every count; hypothetical and off-topic posts excluded; fund-level stops not counted as drop-off; leaving a competitor never counted as leaving Coin; quotes stating drop-off or money capped at their share of posts; forum-collected posts not counted as an answer to where investors go for help.
- Privacy: posts where the author states their own name are never quoted; authors shown only as channel labels.

**Caveats:** Play Store reviews lean negative; several problems draw over 70% of posts from one channel (flagged ⚑); many posts name no platform; three Play reviews filed under Coin are Kite app reviews and are counted as Kite.
