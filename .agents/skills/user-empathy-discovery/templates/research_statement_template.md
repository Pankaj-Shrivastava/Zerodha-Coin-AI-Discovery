## 3. Scope & Non-Goals

Maintain strict boundaries to prevent drifting into premature solutioning:

* **In Scope**:
  - Emotional, psychological, and informational barriers during discovery.
  - Pain points, anxieties, and friction in the evaluation workflow.
  - Manual workarounds, external advice channels, and comparison habits.
  - Language, mental models, and vocabulary used by users.
* **Strict Non-Goals**:
  - Prototyping features, wireframing UI components, or designing screens.
  - Brainstorming technical solutions or architectural implementations.
  - Ideating growth hacks, marketing copy, or promotional campaigns.

---

## 4. Key Exploration Questions

Identify the critical unknowns to be investigated across secondary and primary research:
1. *What specific moment or trigger prompts the user to seek out a mutual fund?*
2. *When presented with a fund listing or search page, what is the user's immediate emotional reaction?*
3. *What specific terminology, numbers, or graphs provoke confusion or fear?*
4. *What makeshift solutions or third-party resources do users consult to overcome this confusion?*
5. *What makes a user abandon the discovery flow at the final hurdle?*
