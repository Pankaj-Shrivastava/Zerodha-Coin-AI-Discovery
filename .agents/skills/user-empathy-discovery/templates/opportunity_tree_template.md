# Template: Opportunity Tree & Prioritization Rubric

Use this template to structure raw user pain points and friction signals into a coherent, hierarchical **Opportunity Tree** (modeled on Teresa Torres' Opportunity Solution Tree methodology) and to size them using Tanguy Crusson's evidence rubric.

---

## 1. Hierarchical Opportunity Tree Structure

```
Target Product Outcome:
[e.g., Increase High-Confidence Discovery & Fund Selection for First-Time Investors]
│
├── 1.0 TOP-LEVEL OPPORTUNITY THEME: [Unmet Customer Need]
│   │
│   ├── 1.1 Sub-Opportunity: [Specific Pain Point / Barrier in User Language]
│   │   ├── Verbatim Evidence / Nugget 1
│   │   ├── Verbatim Evidence / Nugget 2
│   │   └── Root Cause (5 Whys Analysis)
│   │
│   └── 1.2 Sub-Opportunity: [Specific Pain Point / Barrier in User Language]
│       ├── Verbatim Evidence / Nugget 1
│       └── Root Cause (5 Whys Analysis)
│
└── 2.0 TOP-LEVEL OPPORTUNITY THEME: [Unmet Customer Need]
    │
    ├── 2.1 Sub-Opportunity: [Specific Pain Point / Barrier in User Language]
    │   └── Verbatim Evidence / Nugget 1
    │
    └── 2.2 Sub-Opportunity: [Specific Pain Point / Barrier in User Language]
        └── Verbatim Evidence / Nugget 1
```

> [!CAUTION]
> Remember: Opportunities are **unmet needs or pain points expressed from the user's perspective**. 
> They MUST NOT specify solutions (e.g., write *"I don't understand how volatile this fund will be during a downturn"*, NOT *"Add a volatility calculator"*).

---

## 2. Opportunity Sizing & Scoring Rubric

Evaluate each Sub-Opportunity against four objective criteria:

| Opportunity ID & Name | Frequency (Evidence Volume) | Pain Severity (Friction Level) | Emotional Intensity (Psychological Cost) | Strategic Persona Alignment | Overall Opportunity Score (H / M / L) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Opp 1.1**: [e.g., Jargon-induced choice paralysis] | **High** (>60% of reviews/feedback mention technical terms) | **Blocker** (Causes users to close the app without acting) | **Acute** (Feelings of inadequacy and intimidation) | **Core Target** (First-time retail investors) | **HIGH** |
| **Opp 1.2**: [e.g., Mistrust of fund sorting algorithms] | **Medium** (25% mention fear of hidden commissions) | **Friction** (Delays decision; seeks external advice) | **High** (Distrust, cynicism) | **Core Target** | **HIGH** |
| **Opp 2.1**: [e.g., Inability to filter by ESG criteria] | **Low** (<5% mention) | **Annoyance** (Can find manually if needed) | **Low** (Mild inconvenience) | **Peripheral** (Niche cohort) | **LOW** |

### Sizing Definitions:
1. **Frequency**:
   - *High*: Mentioned consistently across >40% of secondary signal sources.
   - *Medium*: Mentioned in 15-40% of sources.
   - *Low*: Mentioned in <15% of sources.
2. **Pain Severity**:
   - *Blocker*: Completely halts the discovery process; leads directly to platform abandonment.
   - *Friction*: Causes significant delay, frustration, or forces complex external workarounds.
   - *Annoyance*: Minor irritation; user still completes the workflow despite the flaw.
3. **Emotional Intensity**:
   - *Acute*: Triggers fear of financial catastrophe, profound embarrassment, or deep cynicism/distrust.
   - *Moderate*: Causes cognitive fatigue, confusion, or impatience.
   - *Mild*: Slight aesthetic or organizational preference.
