query_stats Behavioral Phase Shift

Month 1 exhibits hyper-engagement (daily NAV checks, screenshotting micro-gains). By Month 3, if returns are negative or flat, the user experiences "Loss Aversion Avoidance"—deliberately ignoring notifications, dreading opening Coin, and treating the SIP as an invisible expense rather than an active investment.

"First 30 days I checked Coin after 9:30 PM NAV update every single night. Now it's Month 4, Smallcap fund is down 4%, and I haven't opened the app in three weeks. Just seeing the red icon ruins my day." — TradingQ&A Community User #8112
pause_circle Discontinuation Catalysts

Stoppages are rarely deliberate strategic pivots; they are punitive emotional reactions. An accidental NACH debit failure causing a ₹500 bank penalty induces immense resentment against the mutual fund platform, precipitating immediate cancellation.

Investors monitor current market value against total invested capital, but lack intuitive benchmarking. They obsess over daily point fluctuations instead of unit accumulation volume.

Demat mutual fund mechanics confuse retail users accustomed to instant UPI transactions. When funds leave their bank on Monday but units don't show until Wednesday evening, 41% believe a payment failure occurred.

Because Coin remains intentionally minimalist, users seek emotional shelter in anonymous online forums, exposing them to speculative noise and bad timing advice.

Retained users possess a clear mental model of Rupee Cost Averaging. They celebrate red months because their ₹5,000 monthly allotment purchases more units.

Salaried users churn on psychological disillusionment; freelancers and gig workers churn on rigid debit scheduling that clashes with uneven cash flows.

  • Settlement Status Stepper: Explicit Domino-style visual tracking from Bank Debit → AMC Processing → Demat Allotment.
  • Drawdown Unit Visualizer: Re-anchoring the interface from daily NAV loss to "Bonus Units Acquired at Lower Price".
