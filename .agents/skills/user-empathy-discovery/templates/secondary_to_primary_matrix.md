# Template: Secondary-to-Primary Research Validation Matrix

Use this matrix to bridge **Secondary Research Findings** (discovered by the AI Discovery Engine from reviews, forums, tickets, and domain literature) with **Primary Research Execution** (1-on-1 customer interviews).

The goal is to provide product teams with concrete hypotheses and **story-based interview prompts** to validate or invalidate opportunity areas directly with human users.

## 2. Guidelines for Primary Interviewers (The Interviewer's Pocket Heuristic)

1. **Never Defend the Product**: If the user criticizes or misunderstands something, do not explain how it works. Say: *"That's really interesting—tell me more about what that made you think."*
2. **Anchor in Past Episodes**: If the interviewee starts hypothesizing (*"Usually, I think I would..."*), immediately anchor them back into reality: *"Can you tell me about a specific time when that actually happened?"*
3. **Probe the Workaround**: Whenever a user mentions asking someone else or checking another site, drill deeper: *"What did you search for? What did they tell you? Why did you trust their answer over what was on the screen?"*
4. **Log Emotional Markers**: Note changes in tone, sighs, laughter (often masking embarrassment), and pauses.
